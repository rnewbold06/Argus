## Writing entries

For every changed part, state:

1. **Which STL revision** (`argus2_top_bracket_v3.stl` → `_v4`)
2. **What changed** and why
3. **Compatibility** — does the new part fit hardware built from the previous
   revision, or does something else need reprinting alongside it?
4. **Whether rig geometry changed.** If camera offsets moved, say so loudly.
   Anyone using KapturPlan defaults needs to update their numbers.
